The GoGenius Manual

GoGenius is the first app designed to teach European Portuguese, especially to speakers of nine major languages, but it can be used by anyone interested in learning. Through themed games covering listening, reading, and writing, this guide walks you through every screen and button.

Before you start For the best experience, plug in your headphones or connect Bluetooth headphones to your phone or tablet before you open GoGenius. Make sure audio is playing through those headphones — many games depend on listening, and clear sound helps you follow prompts and pronunciation activities without distraction.

GoGenius is designed for immigrants and refugees living in Portugal, foreign learners of Portuguese anywhere, and schools using it as a classroom tool. It runs on Android phones, tablets, and computers (via an Android emulator on PC). A single download unlocks every module — there is no subscription.

GoGenius is also built to be inclusive: every game includes a written version of its goal and instructions for deaf and hard-of-hearing learners, and any item can be replayed in your own language or in European Portuguese whenever you need it.

The educational model was created by PhD Sandra Figueiredo

Get started · 60 seconds
Onboarding in a minute
  • Five minutes a day is enough. Complete the day's tasks in about five minutes — keep that up and you can move from A2 toward B1 in roughly two months.
  • Learn together. The exercises work just as well done in a group as on your own.
  • Made for the classroom. Teachers can run the games in class and turn them into a quiz for the whole group.

01Launch screen

When you open GoGenius, you're greeted by the welcome screen — the logo over a warm ribbon of “hello” in many languages. After a moment it takes you to language selection.

02Signing in & registering

Next comes the Bem-Vindo! (Welcome!) screen. If you already have an account, sign in here; if this is your first time, create one.

If you already have an account

Enter your E-mail and Password, then tap Entrar (Log in). Tap the eye icon to check what you've typed. Forgotten your password? Tap Recuperar Password (Recover password) to reset it by e-mail.

If you're new — register

Tap Registar (Register) to open the sign-up form (Entrar com o E-mail). Fill in your details and tap Registar to finish.

03Choosing your language

Pick the language you already speak. The prompt appears in both Portuguese and English — Escolhe a tua língua / Select your language.

Tap the ‹ and › arrows to cycle through the flags until you reach yours, then tap Ok. GoGenius supports nine source languages: English, Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, Russian, Romanian, Ukrainian, German, and French.

Each of these nine languages is a support language — it's there to help you follow the instructions, while everything you're actually learning stays in European Portuguese. That focus is what sets GoGenius apart: most Portuguese-learning apps teach the Brazilian variant, which differs noticeably in pronunciation, vocabulary, and grammar, and many language games aren't built for real teaching at all. GoGenius was designed specifically as an educational tool for European Portuguese.

Why the interface stays in Portuguese Your choice sets the language used for instructions and translations — but the buttons and menu names stay in Portuguese on purpose. Finding your way around a Portuguese interface is part of the immersion, and the words you pick up from the menus (perfil, categorias, atualizar) are words you'll use in real life. This manual gives you the English meaning of every button as you go.

07Categorias — themes

Categorias is the heart of GoGenius: the 14 themed games that make up the curriculum. Each theme takes an everyday topic and builds a set of exercises around it. Some of the themes you'll find include, but are not limited to:

A minha gente · My family Onde moro · Where I live O que visto · What I wear Movendo-me · Getting around Natura · Nature Eu, Hoje e no Futuro · Me, today & the future

Whatever theme you enter, the learning is organised around the same three skill areas — the real structure of the app. Each trains a different competency and hands you the solutions as you go:

Graph
Writing

Exercícios de Escrita — writing activities, with worked solutions.

Lex
Reading

Exercícios de Leitura — reading practice in European Portuguese, with solutions.

Fonê
Oral comprehension

Exercícios de Compreensão Oral — listening and phonology activities.

You'll meet these three names again inside every game and on your scoreboard, where your progress in each is tracked (see Classificações). The next section walks through a whole theme so you can see them in action.

08Themed games — a walkthrough

This section follows an example of a themed game you can choose, A minha gente, from the carousel to the exercises, so you can see how a game actually works.

1 · Browse and open a theme

Categorias opens as a winding carousel of category circles. Swipe along the path to move between themes and tap a circle to open one; the tab that slides out on the right (here, A minha gente) shows which theme you're about to enter. The two small tablet icons in the top-right corner are a language switch (explained further down), and the red question-mark button opens an index of what every button in the app does — keep it handy as a reference.

The index is a handy legend: it names the three exercise types — Graph (writing), Lex (reading) and Fonê (oral comprehension) — alongside Sair (exit), Solução (solution), Saber mais (more info), Info, the hearing-impairment accessibility icon, and the two language menus (Menu em Português Europeu and Menu Escolher Outras Línguas).

2 · Pick a game

Close the index and tap the A minha gente theme. A small menu of that theme's games appears — an introductory game plus Graph, Lex and Fonê, each with its own Jogar (Play) button. You can play them in any order, but it's best to start with the heart button — the introductory game.

3 · Inside a game

Here's what the introductory game looks like in play. Drag and drop the bottom tabs on the corresponding person (Pai, Avô, Filhos/Irmãos, Mãe, Avó) to work through the family vocabulary. Two round buttons sit in the bottom-right corner, and the two tablet icons in the top-right switch the language (see the note below).

The + button opens a Vocabulário panel listing every word the activity will teach — a handy reference before you begin. The ear button shows the activity's goal and instructions as text: it's an accessibility feature (headed Portador de Deficiência Auditiva, “person with a hearing impairment”) so deaf and hard-of-hearing learners can read exactly what to do.

At any moment during an activity or game, look at the bottom centre of the screen for a small upward-pointing arrow. Tap it to open a quick menu that jumps to Perfil, Categorias, Tradução, or back to the main Menu — so you can move between those sections without hunting through the app.

Left: the + button’s Vocabulário list  ·  Right: the ear button’s text instructions.

Hear it again — in your language or in Portuguese Every game is bilingual. In the top-right corner you'll see two small tablet icons. Tap the globe tablet (Menu Escolher Outras Línguas) to hear or read the current item again in your own language, or the Portugal-flag tablet (Menu em Português Europeu) to switch it back to European Portuguese. Go back and forth as often as you need — the app never locks you into one language.

4 · Graph, Lex & Fonê

After the introductory game, you move on to the theme's three skill exercises — Graph (writing), Lex (reading) and Fonê (oral comprehension).

Graph focuses on your writing skills. Here's how it works in the A minha gente theme:

Once again, the two tablet icons in the upper-right corner let you hear the activity instructions: the world-map button plays them in your native language, and the Portugal-flag button plays them in Portuguese.

After hearing the instructions, tap the peach area where you see A tua resposta... (“Your answer...”) and begin writing. Adding a Portuguese keyboard on your device is strongly recommended — on iPhone, for example, you can enable a Portuguese layout so accents, grammar, and spelling stay correct as you type.

Left: keyboard open to type  ·  Right: response ready — tap the blue checkmark to submit.

When you finish writing, tap the blue checkmark. Then compare your answer with the solution: look to the lower-right corner and tap the red lightbulb. A Solução panel appears with a model response.

Match the meaning, not every word Don’t worry if your response and the solution vary. What matters is that they share the same meaning, solid grammar and syntax, and convey the same message.

Lex is reading practice in European Portuguese, with support built in. As before, the two tablet icons in the upper-right let you hear the activity instructions in your mother tongue (world map) or in Portuguese (Portugal flag).

Left: the reading prompt  ·  Right: Dicionário open with a keyword explained.

You are given a short text to read and understand. Read it out loud — that strengthens conversational skill, pronunciation, and the sound of European Portuguese.

Below the prompt is an orange Dicionário (Dictionary) tab. Tap it to expand key words and phrases from the text, explained in Portuguese — useful when a term is new.

The red lightbulb in the lower-right corner plays the prompt aloud in Portuguese as soon as you tap it. Use it as a model for how the text should sound, then practise reading along.

Read, hear, pronounce — then repeat The goal of Lex is reading, listening, and pronouncing in the European Portuguese. Practise often, and always read the prompt out loud.

Fonê works on oral comprehension — Exercícios de Compreensão Oral: listening and phonology activities.

Once again, the two tablet icons in the upper-right corner let you hear the activity instructions in your native language (world map) or in European Portuguese (Portugal flag).

Left: ready to listen  ·  Right: tap the white disk to start the conversation.

Tap the white disk beside Questões to play the conversation you will use as a reference for the questions below. Once selected, the disk turns a little grey and the conversation begins.

When you finish listening, play it again two or three times until you understand it, then mark the correct answers (Assinale a resposta correta).

Questions stay in European Portuguese These reflection questions are always in European Portuguese. Keep Google Translate or your favourite translator app ready — we translate this first module for you below, but later you’ll do that yourself. It’s a good habit to build with translator apps or your own notes. You can also open Tradução from the bottom arrow menu.

Listen more than once Oral comprehension improves with repetition. Replay the disk, check the Dicionário, and use the ear transcript whenever you need a second look at the dialogue.

5 · Beyond this walkthrough

What you just followed in A minha gente applies to any theme or activity in GoGenius, with only small variations. The activities are largely self-explanatory, and you already have the tools for success: bilingual instructions, the bottom arrow menu, Dicionário, solutions, accessibility text, and Tradução when you need a hand.

If something in the app is unclear, come back to this manual — or reach out through Support & contact. We’re here to help you on this journey of learning European Portuguese.

Boa sorte! · Good luck Enjoy the games — and keep practising.

09Classificações — scores & rankings

Reached through Perfil ▸ Classificações, this is your scoreboard. It lists every theme in the app; tap one to expand it and see how you've done.

Each expanded theme shows a star rating (out of five) plus a completion badge for each of the three skills — Graph, Lex, and Fonê. A green check means you've finished that skill's exercises for the theme.

How points are earned Stars and points are awarded only when you complete all the tasks in a game — partial completion doesn't count, because a finished game is treated as evidence you've actually learned the material. If you can't finish in one sitting, just leave it and pick it up later; nothing is lost.

10Sobre a APP & privacy

Also under Perfil, the Sobre a APP / Políticas de Privacidade screen holds background on the app and its full privacy policy. Tap the Portuguese or English flag at the top to switch languages.

In short: GoGenius supports assisted learning of European Portuguese through reading, writing, and phonetic exercises, backed by tutorials in several languages. As you play, a classification grid is built so you can see your proficiency. The app is available on Android (and on PC via an emulator), and the research team suggests short daily sessions of 15–20 minutes for best results.

The policy is also online at privacidade.grupoautonoma.pt.
